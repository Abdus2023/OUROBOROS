# M1.24 — Recovery-Quorum Signer Identity Non-Reuse

## Status

Implemented hardening slice.

## Problem

M1.23 requires historical recovery-quorum signer identifiers to be non-reusable. The current quorum membership alone is insufficient to enforce that rule: a signer removed at epoch N could otherwise be reintroduced at a later epoch under the same identifier with fresh key material.

That would make signer identity an epoch-local label rather than a durable lifecycle identity and would weaken replay reasoning.

## Rule

During authoritative quorum lifecycle replay:

```text
historical signer IDs
        ∪
current signer IDs
        ↓
identity reservation set
```

For every `ROTATE_SIGNER`, the replacement identifier must not occur in the reservation set. Key material must remain fresh as already required by M1.23.

A removed signer therefore remains retired forever for the lifetime of that quorum lineage.

## Why replay is the authority boundary

`sign_lifecycle_statement()` validates the current externally authorized quorum, but it does not possess the complete historical journal. The complete no-reuse decision therefore belongs to ordered durable replay, where the full lifecycle history is available.

The authoritative sequence is:

```text
external genesis quorum
        ↓
ordered lifecycle event
        ↓
current quorum verification
        ↓
historical identifier check
        ↓
apply transition
        ↓
reserve all observed identifiers
```

A statement may therefore be structurally and cryptographically valid in isolation while still being rejected by authoritative replay because its replacement identifier was used by an earlier quorum epoch.

## Regression coverage

The conformance suite now covers:

1. initial signer `q2` rotated out for `q3`;
2. `q3` rotated out for `q4`;
3. a later signed transition attempts to introduce the historical identifier `q2` again;
4. ordered recovery rejects the event with `signer id was previously used`.

Existing M1.23 tests were also aligned with the fail-closed signing boundary: impossible threshold transitions and stale signer use are rejected while constructing the statement rather than deferred to a later verification call.

## Invariant

> A recovery-quorum signer identifier is a globally unique lifecycle identity within its externally rooted quorum lineage and cannot be reused after retirement.
